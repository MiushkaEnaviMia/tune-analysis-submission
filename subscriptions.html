What each split means

  • Free trial or paid: few trial users pay later: 12.9% and 4.8% of the users in the two free-trial waves
  • Plan length: longer plans pay upfront: 3M ARPPU is $1,749 on Annual vs $421 on Monthly Auto-renew
  • Payment type, Monthly only: the strongest driver of churn: 19.9% per renewal on Manual vs 3.0% on Auto-renew
  • Price point: $99 brings less at first, but caught up with $149 by month 12 ($1,160 vs $1,159), because 92% still paid a year later vs 54%
  • Discount, $149 Auto-renew only: 9.7% of paid purchases had one in Jan 2015; discounts ended in Nov 2015 and the cheaper $99 plan took their place
